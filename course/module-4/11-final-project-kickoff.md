# Lesson 4.11 — Final Project Kickoff: Pick Your Idea

## The Big Idea

Everything you've learned in this course has been building toward this.

You know how to code the basics. You know how AI works. You know how to use AI tools to build things fast. Now you're going to use all of that to make something real.

The best final projects solve a problem you actually care about — not a fake textbook problem, a real one from your own life.

---

## Do This First

Write down 3 app ideas. For each one, write:

- **Who uses it?** (You? Your friends? Your family? Your school?)
- **What problem does it solve?** (One sentence.)
- **Why would they actually use it?** (Would you use it today if it existed?)

Don't filter yourself yet. Just write. Dumb ideas are fine at this stage.

When you're done, read all three. Pick the one that makes you think "I actually want this to exist."

---

## Some Examples to Get You Started

You don't have to use these. They're just to get ideas moving.

- **Homework tracker** — Enter assignments, set reminders, mark done. You'd use it every day.
- **Vibe playlist maker** — Describe your mood, get a Spotify playlist recommendation.
- **Study flashcard generator** — Paste your notes, get flashcards back automatically.
- **Chore split app** — Track who's done their chores in your house. Everyone can see it.
- **Random activity picker** — When you're bored and can't decide what to do.

---

## What Makes a Good Final Project

**It solves a real problem.**
Not "I thought this might be cool" — more like "I actually need this."

**You can build a working version.**
Doesn't need every feature. It needs to do the core thing. One job, done well.

**You can explain it in one sentence.**
If it takes a paragraph to explain what your app does, it's too complicated for a final project.

---

## What You Don't Need

You don't need:

- A unique idea (most ideas have been done before — your version is still yours)
- A business plan
- Design skills
- Every feature figured out now

You just need an idea you want to build.

Pick an idea that solves a real problem for real people — even if those people are just you and your friends. Write it down. That's your final project.

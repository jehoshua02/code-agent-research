# Lesson 1.01 — Computers Are Dumb (On Purpose)

## The Big Idea

Computers are not smart. They are fast, but they are not smart.

A computer does exactly what you tell it to do. Nothing more. Nothing less. If you give it bad instructions, it follows the bad instructions perfectly and gets the wrong answer.

This is not a bug. This is the whole point.

## Think About It This Way

Imagine you told a friend: "Grab me a drink from the fridge."

A human friend would figure out what you meant, open the fridge, and grab you something to drink. Easy.

Now imagine you said that to a robot. The robot would stop and ask: What fridge? Where is it? How do I open it? What counts as a drink? Which one do I grab?

Computers are like that robot. They need exact instructions. They will not guess. They will not assume. They will not "figure it out."

This sounds annoying — but it's actually what makes computers useful. Because once you give them the right instructions, they follow those instructions the same way every single time, instantly, billions of times if needed.

## Why This Matters

Every bug you will ever see in software is a case of:
- The programmer said one thing
- The computer did exactly that thing
- But the programmer meant something different

The computer was not wrong. The instructions were wrong.

Once you understand this, you stop being frustrated at computers. You start asking: "What did I actually tell it to do?"

## Activity: Tell the Computer to Remember Your Name

Let's prove this right now.

**Step 1:** Press the Windows key, search for "Notepad," and open it.

**Step 2:** Type your first name. Just your name, nothing else.

**Step 3:** Go to File > Save As.

**Step 4:** Name the file `myname.txt` and save it to your Desktop.

That's it. You just created a file. A real file. The computer saved exactly what you typed — not more, not less.

Open the file again. It still says your name. The computer did not change it, add to it, or decide it knew better. It saved exactly what you told it to save.

That is a computer doing its job.

## Quick Recap

- Computers follow instructions exactly.
- They do not guess or assume.
- This makes them predictable — which is what makes them powerful.
- Every bug is the programmer giving the wrong instructions.

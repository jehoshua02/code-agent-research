# Lesson 2.07 — Temperature: How Random Is the AI?

## Try this first

Open two separate ChatGPT tabs.

In both, ask: **"Write me a two-sentence story about a dragon."**

Read both answers. Are they exactly the same?

Probably not. Maybe slightly different words, different dragon, different vibe.

That's **temperature** in action.

---

## What is temperature?

Temperature is a setting that controls how predictable or creative the AI's output is.

Think of it like a dial:

- **Low temperature** = AI picks the most obvious, safe word every time. Consistent, boring, predictable.
- **High temperature** = AI picks from a wider range of words, sometimes surprising ones. Creative, varied, occasionally weird.

---

## The ice cream shop analogy

Imagine you walk into an ice cream shop and ask for a recommendation.

A low-temperature employee always says "vanilla." Safe, classic, never wrong.

A high-temperature employee might say "lavender honey with pretzel bits" — interesting, sometimes great, sometimes a miss.

Same job. Very different outputs.

---

## Why does this matter?

Different tasks need different temperatures.

| Task | Best temperature |
|------|-----------------|
| Math or code | Low — you want exact, reliable answers |
| Creative writing | High — you want variety and surprise |
| Summarizing facts | Low — accuracy matters |
| Brainstorming ideas | High — more ideas, more range |

Most AI tools pick a default temperature. But some let you adjust it.

---

## You already saw this happen

Remember in Lesson 2.01 when you asked "The sky is" and got back an answer?

If you asked the same thing 10 times, you'd get slightly different completions. That's because the model doesn't always pick the single most likely word — it picks from a weighted list, with some randomness mixed in.

Temperature controls how much randomness gets added.

---

## The key idea

> Temperature controls how creative or predictable the AI is. Low temperature = reliable. High temperature = surprising.

You can't usually change this directly in ChatGPT, but it's always running in the background.

---

## Activity

In ChatGPT, ask the same question five times in a row in the same chat:

**"Give me a one-sentence description of the ocean."**

Keep clicking send without changing anything.

**How different are the five answers? Write down the most interesting one.**

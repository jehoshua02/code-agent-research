# Lesson 2.08 — Training vs. Knowing

## Try this first

Ask ChatGPT about something that happened very recently — like a game result from last week, a new movie that just dropped, or something in the news today.

What happened? Did it answer confidently? Say it doesn't know? Make something up?

---

## AI was trained, not updated

Here's something easy to miss: AI doesn't browse the internet in real time.

It was trained on a huge pile of text up to a certain date. After that date — called the **training cutoff** — it learned nothing new.

It's like a really smart friend who read every book and article ever written... but stopped reading in, say, 2024. They'd know a ton. But they'd have no idea what happened last month.

---

## Training vs. knowing

These sound the same, but they're different.

**Knowing** means you can look something up, check a source, or verify a fact right now.

**Training** means patterns got baked into the model during a one-time process. The model doesn't look things up. It recalls patterns from what it was trained on.

If something happened after the cutoff, there are no patterns for it. The model genuinely doesn't have it.

---

## So why does it sometimes answer anyway?

Because it predicts words.

If you ask about a recent event, the model might make something up that sounds plausible — because plausible-sounding answers are what it's trained to produce.

This is called a **hallucination**. (More on that next lesson.)

---

## The cutoff date varies

Different models have different cutoff dates.

ChatGPT might know things up to early 2024. A newer model might know things up to late 2024. It depends on when training happened.

Some AI tools (like Perplexity) connect to live search to get around this. But base models like ChatGPT don't — they're frozen in time.

---

## The key idea

> AI was trained up to a cutoff date. It doesn't know what happened after that — and it might confidently make things up when you ask.

Always check AI answers about recent events with a real source.

---

## Activity

Ask ChatGPT these questions and note what happens:

- "Who won the Super Bowl this year?"
- "What's the latest iPhone model?"
- "What happened in the news today?"

**Did it answer, refuse, or guess? What clues did it give that it might not actually know?**

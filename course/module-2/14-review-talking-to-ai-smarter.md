## The most important things to remember

**1. AI is predicting, not thinking.**
Every answer is a word-by-word prediction. That explains the confidence, the mistakes, and the weirdness.

**2. Better prompts = better outputs.**
You're not just asking questions. You're programming an output. Be specific.

**3. Always verify facts.**
AI is not a search engine. It hallucinates. Double-check anything important.

**4. Different tools for different jobs.**
ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity — they're not interchangeable. Pick based on what you need.

## The final activity

Write the best prompt you can.

Pick any topic you actually care about — a game, a hobby, something you want to learn, a project you're working on.

Write a prompt that includes:
- What you want to know
- Who you are and why it matters (age, skill level, context)
- What format you want (list, paragraph, short, detailed)
- Any specific instructions (simple language, funny tone, examples)

Send it to ChatGPT. Read the result.

**Save this prompt somewhere. It's the start of your prompt library.**

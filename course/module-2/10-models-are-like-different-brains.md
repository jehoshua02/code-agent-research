# Lesson 2.10 — Models Are Like Different Brains

## Try this first

Pick a simple coding question. Something like:

**"Write a function in Python that checks if a number is even."**

Ask it on **chat.openai.com** (ChatGPT).

Then open **claude.ai** and ask the same thing.

Compare the two answers. Different style? Different length? Different explanation?

---

## Not all AI is the same

When people say "AI," they usually mean a specific **model**.

A model is a particular trained system — built by a specific company, on specific data, with specific goals.

Some common ones:

| Model | Company |
|-------|---------|
| GPT-4o | OpenAI |
| Claude | Anthropic |
| Gemini | Google |
| Llama | Meta |

These are not the same thing. They have different strengths, different styles, and different costs.

---

## Think of it like different people

GPT-4o is like the fast, confident friend who always has an answer — even if it's not always perfect.

Claude is more like the careful, thoughtful friend who tends to explain things clearly and hedge when unsure.

Gemini is Google's take — connected to their products, often pulling from search.

None of them is "the best" at everything. They each shine in different areas.

---

## Speed and cost matter too

Bigger, smarter models are slower and more expensive to run.

That's why companies offer multiple versions:

- A fast, cheap model for simple tasks.
- A powerful, expensive model for complex tasks.

If you're building something with AI, picking the right model for the job matters. You don't use a sports car to deliver groceries.

---

## Why use different models?

- **Coding** — some models are better at writing and debugging code.
- **Creative writing** — some have a better sense of style and voice.
- **Summarizing** — some are faster and cheaper for simple tasks.
- **Safety and accuracy** — some are more cautious with sensitive topics.

---

## The key idea

> GPT-4, Claude, Gemini — each model has different strengths, speeds, and costs. Knowing which tool to pick is part of using AI well.

---

## Activity

Ask the same question to two different AI tools:

**"Explain how the internet works in 3 sentences."**

Use ChatGPT and Claude (claude.ai — free to use).

**Compare: Which one was clearer? Which one was shorter? Which one felt more like talking to a person?**

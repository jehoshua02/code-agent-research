# Lesson 2.04 — Context Window: AI's Working Memory

## Try this first

Open a new ChatGPT chat. Type:

**"My name is [your name]. Remember that."**

Have a normal conversation. Ask it stuff. Do this for about 20 messages.

Then ask: **"What's my name?"**

Did it remember? Or did it forget?

---

## AI has a memory limit

Your brain stores things long-term. You remember your name, your friends, what happened last year.

AI doesn't work like that.

AI has a **context window** — a fixed amount of text it can see at once. Think of it like a piece of paper. Everything on the paper, it can read. Anything that falls off the edge, it forgets.

---

## RAM, not a hard drive

Here's a good way to think about it:

- Your **hard drive** stores everything long-term. Even when you turn off your computer, the files are still there.
- Your **RAM** (working memory) only holds what you're using right now. When you close the program, it's gone.

AI has RAM. Not a hard drive.

Every new conversation starts blank. It doesn't remember you from last time. It only remembers what's in the current chat — and even that has a limit.

---

## What happens when the window fills up?

Imagine the paper fills up. New stuff gets added at the bottom. Old stuff at the top slides off.

The AI literally can no longer see the earlier parts of your conversation.

That's why, in a very long chat, AI might seem to "forget" what you said at the start.

---

## How big is the window?

It depends on the model. But to give you an idea:

- A small window might hold a few pages of text.
- A large window might hold hundreds of pages.

More isn't always better. Bigger windows cost more to run.

---

## The key idea

> AI can only "remember" what's in its context window — the current conversation. Once text falls outside that window, it's gone.

This is why long conversations can get weird, and why every new chat starts fresh.

---

## Activity

Start a new ChatGPT chat. Tell it three made-up facts about yourself:

- A fake pet name
- A fake favorite food
- A fake hometown

Keep chatting for 10+ messages about other stuff.

Then ask: "What do you know about me?"

**Did it remember all three? Write down what it got right and wrong.**
